FoldMatch

Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

The short version: FoldMatch does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Your progress stays on your device and in your own iCloud account.

FoldMatch is a puzzle game for iPhone published by Ian Kehayes, operating as Harbour Hues Media, in Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

Information we collect

None. FoldMatch has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking tools. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your level progress and settings, such as sound and appearance, on your iPhone so you can pick up where you left off.

If you're signed in to iCloud, your level progress also syncs through your own iCloud account so it carries over between your devices. Apple stores that copy in your account; we can't see or access it. You can turn this off for FoldMatch in your iPhone's iCloud settings.

Device sensors

On iPhone Duo, FoldMatch reads the angle of the hinge so you can play by folding the phone. This reading is used in the moment to run the game. It is not saved or sent anywhere.

Purchases

If FoldMatch offers in-app purchases, they are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment details. Apple's handling of that information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

FoldMatch is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Sharing

We have no personal information to sell, rent, or share with anyone.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of FoldMatch changes how information is handled, we will update this page and the date at the top before that version is released.
