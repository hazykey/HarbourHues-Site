Common questions

Which iPhones does Fold Match work on?

Fold Match is built for iPhone Duo, where you play by folding the phone itself. It also works on other iPhones running a supported iOS version, using an on-screen slider in place of the fold.

How do I play?

Each level shows a target shape. On iPhone Duo, open or close the phone until your shape mirrors the target. On other iPhones, drag the slider to do the same. Match it closely enough and the crease locks in.

The fold isn't being detected on my iPhone Duo
  1. Close Fold Match completely and reopen it.
  2. Make sure your iPhone is updated to the latest iOS version.
  3. Remove any case that limits how far the phone opens.
  4. Still stuck? Email us with your iOS version.
Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your device and synced through your iCloud account, so it carries over to a new iPhone and comes back if you reinstall Fold Match. Make sure you're signed in to iCloud and that iCloud is turned on for Fold Match in Settings.

How do I restore a purchase?

If you bought an unlock and it isn't showing, open Fold Match's settings and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does Fold Match collect my data?

No. There are no accounts, ads, or tracking. Read the full privacy policy.
